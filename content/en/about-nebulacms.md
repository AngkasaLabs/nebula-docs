# About NebulaCMS

NebulaCMS is an open-source content management system built on **Laravel**, with a **React**-based admin and **Blade**-driven public sites. It is designed for teams that want a **single PHP stack**, **clear extension points** (themes and plugins), and a **documented JSON API**—without mixing unrelated runtimes or deployment models.

## What the project optimizes for

- **One coherent stack** — Backend, admin, and theming stay in familiar Laravel and PHP tooling; operations follow common Laravel practices (env config, Artisan, queues, scheduler).
- **Extensibility** — **Hooks** (actions and filters), theme templates, and plugins with a manifest-driven lifecycle.
- **Modern authoring** — A focused admin for posts, pages, media, users, and settings, built with Inertia and modern frontend components.
- **Interoperability** — A **REST API** for published content so you can integrate mobile apps, static sites, or automation.

The goal is not to replicate every feature of other CMS products; it is to offer a **maintainable, developer-friendly** foundation for content sites that fit the Laravel ecosystem.

## Documentation languages

- **English** — Fully maintained; it is the primary documentation language today.
- **Indonesian** — Planned as the next locale; pages will be added incrementally.
- **Other languages** — May follow over time as the project and community grow.

Contributions and translations are welcome as the localization structure matures.

## History and direction

The project has evolved with its contributors and users. Roadmaps and release notes on the **[GitHub repository](https://github.com/AngkasaLabs/nebula-cms)** are the best place to follow **what ships when** and how to get involved.
